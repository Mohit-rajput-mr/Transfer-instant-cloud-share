# Orbi Transfer — "The airport" (79 s, 1080×1920, house style, voice bm_george)

`OrbiTransfer-airport.mp4` · cover `cover.jpg`. Low-poly colour men and women (people style 5, `../people-samples/`).
Friends land after a week in Lisbon; everyone wants the trip photos. Instead of email batches or blurry chat squares you
drop the whole folder into Orbi with code "tram" and shout it across the gate. Then the luggage metaphor, layer by layer:
photos packed into a suitcase locked with your code (the code stays with you), split into small bags riding several belts
at once, waiting in a sealed baggage hold, Orbi pinging your part of the city like a radar arrivals board (never your seat),
your friend types the code, bags roll in on the carousel, snap together and unpack, and 30 minutes later the hold is
shredded. Rebuild: see `../VIDEO_PLAYBOOK.md`.
