# Why the cold locks your charge (science film)
3D cutaway film on the kit (`source/`), tyre-film v2 quality. Beats: phone says 40% then dies in cold → cut a lithium cell:
graphite anode / metal-oxide cathode / separator / liquid electrolyte → charge = lithium ions tucked in the graphite →
each ion crossing pushes an electron round your phone = current → warm: thin electrolyte, ions swim, bright screen →
cold: electrolyte thickens like fridge honey, ions crawl and stall at the surface → voltage sags, drops below the phone's
cutoff under load, phone shuts off → the charge never left, warm it and it wakes → charging cold = lithium plating (metal
piles on the surface, permanent) → EVs lose winter range, phone revives in a warm pocket → Orbi: file sealed on your phone,
~1 s, no login/app, 30-min burn.
