# Inside a candle (100 s, 1080×1920) · educational, v2 of candle-film

Zoom into a candle → a glass column of price shelves (order book): sell orders wait above, buy orders below →
a buyer's market orders eat the cheapest sells; a cleared shelf steps the price up and fills the candle green →
sellers eat the bids, the green drains, past the open it closes red → the wick = highest shelf reached →
liquidity: a big player can't fill 1,000,000 shares from 40,000 on the shelves → stops under the obvious low are
sells waiting → sweep: push through, stops fire into the big player's (purple) bids → filled, price rips back →
upside down: short sellers' stops above highs are buys → spot it: fast wick through a level, close back inside → tips.
