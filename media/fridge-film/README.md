# Your fridge doesn't make cold (science film)
3D cutaway film on the kit (`source/`), tyre-film v2 quality. Beats: a fridge moves heat, the back is warm → cut open: steel /
foam / plastic walls → one sealed loop (compressor, condenser zigzag, capillary tube, evaporator plate) → refrigerant (often
isobutane, boils at about −12 °C) → boiling takes heat (sweat) → it boils in the cold plate, heat leaves the food → the
compressor squeezes the vapour hot (bike pump) → heat flows hot→cold, out the back, vapour turns liquid → the thin pipe drops
the pressure (spray can) → round and round → thermostat (4 °C / −18 °C, the hum) → open door warms the kitchen → door sticks
(air cools, shrinks; magnetic seal) → it moves heat, stores nothing → Orbi: locked on your phone, ~1 s, no login/app, 30-min burn.
