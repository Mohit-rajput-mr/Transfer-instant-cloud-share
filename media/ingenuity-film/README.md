# Wright Brothers Field: Ingenuity on Mars (HD film, 9:16)

"Do you know a piece of the Wright brothers' first airplane has flown on Mars?" NASA's Ingenuity: 1.8 kg; a small piece of the
1903 Wright Flyer's wing cloth under its solar panel; Mars air ~1/100 as dense (like 26 km up on Earth); blades up to 2,900 rpm
(~10× Earth); coaxial contra-rotating rotors (like the Wright Flyer's two props); minutes of radio delay, so it flew itself;
first flight 19 April 2021, 3 m, ~40 s — the first powered, controlled flight on another world, at "Wright Brothers Field";
planned 5 flights, flew 72, until its blades broke in 2024. Orbi ending (new): direct phone-to-phone first, storage fallback.
