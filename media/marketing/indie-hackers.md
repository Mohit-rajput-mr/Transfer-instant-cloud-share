## Post 1: launch / building in public (post in "Show IH" or the main feed)

**Title:** I built a file drop with no login that burns your files after 30 minutes

Hey IH 👋

For months I kept doing the same dumb thing: emailing files to myself just to move them from my laptop to the PC next to me. AirDrop doesn't work across Windows/Android, and WeTransfer wants an email and keeps files for days.

So I built **Orbitransfer** (orbitransfer.com):

- Drop a file, folder, note or link: it shows up on the screens around you in about a second
- No account, no app, works in any browser
- Encrypted in your browser before it leaves; the server only holds scrambled bytes
- Everything burns after 30 minutes

Then teams started using it, so I added **Orbi Work** (orbitransfer.com/work): one board with files, notes and tasks. You can name a task ("Richard's task"), give it a route with your own coloured steps, upload files straight into it, and everyone sees it move live. There's also an anonymous mode and private spaces with a passcode.

What I learned so far:
1. "No login" is the feature people mention first, not encryption.
2. Short educational videos (how QR codes work, how passwords are stored) brought far more visitors than feature demos.
3. Teams want structure (tasks, routes), not just transfer.

I'd love brutal feedback:
- Is the "files burn" idea a plus or a worry for you?
- What would make you use it with your team?

Thanks for reading 🙏

## Post 2: lessons (a few days later)

**Title:** What I learned making explainer videos to grow a tiny SaaS for $0

I have no ad budget. What worked: 60–100 second science videos with a suspense hook ("Where do your photos actually go when you send them?"), a real answer, and only a 5-second mention of my product at the end.

- The hook has to be a question people can't answer themselves
- The answer must be true and educational: people share learning, not ads
- Keep the product in the last few seconds

Happy to share how I make them cheaply if anyone is interested.
