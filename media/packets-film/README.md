# Orbi Transfer — "Where your photos go" (99 s, 1080×1920, science film, expressive George voice)

`OrbiTransfer-where-photos-go.mp4` · cover `cover.jpg`. Format 2 (see `../VIDEO_PLAYBOOK.md`): explain an everyday
thing, then connect it to Orbi. You photograph your friend in a low-poly garden and send it to your mum across the world.
The photo shatters into 2,000 pixel cubes → 24 million ones and zeros → about 2,000 envelopes (packets) with labels
(from, to, number) → radio waves to the router → hop by hop across a low-poly map → through a sea cable as thick as a
garden hose at ⅔ the speed of light → mum's phone sorts them, asks again for the lost #1337, glues them back.
Then: they pass machines you don't own, so Orbi locks the file on your phone first; big parts, several at once, only
the missing part again; rough area, no account; 30-minute burn. Rebuild: `node render.cjs`, `make_events.py`, `synth.py`.
