# Do you know what happens to your body in space without a suit? (HD body film, 2:31)
Real anatomy (BodyParts3D), real scanned head (Lee Perry-Smith, CC BY 3.0), NASA Earth textures, a station module from
Poly Haven CC0 textures/props. Beats: you won't explode or freeze, ~15 s → airlock, air presses ~1 kg/cm², door opens →
x-ray lungs: don't hold your breath, breathe out → gas bubbles in vessels → water boils at body temperature (6.3 kPa,
above ~19 km), saliva fizzes → skin swells but holds; heart keeps blood under pressure → lungs pull O₂ out, ~15 s,
blackout → cold but empty (thermal view) → unfiltered UV → back to air within ~90 s → Jim LeBlanc, NASA 1966 vacuum
chamber → a spacesuit is a tiny planet → Orbi ad: anonymous spaces (4-letter code + passcode that never leaves the
browser, timer 5–30 min, then it burns), phone + laptop floating in the module. Sound: `space_sfx.py`.
