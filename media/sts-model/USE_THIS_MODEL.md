# Space Shuttle Discovery (OV-103) — USE THIS MODEL

Real 3D scan by the Smithsonian (National Air and Space Museum, Udvar-Hazy Center), 3d.si.edu, Smithsonian Open Access, **CC0**,
with its own photo texture ("Discovery", "United States", flag, NASA logo, tiles). Credit: "Space Shuttle Discovery (OV-103), 3D scan by the Smithsonian Institution (3d.si.edu), CC0".

## File (media/kit/models/discovery/)
- `discovery-150k-4096.glb` (14.6 MB), metres, y up, nose toward **+z**, centred; landing gear down.

## Transform
Apply node matrices, then `translate(0, 7.913, 0)` → wheels on y = 0. Span 23.8 m (x), length 37.6 m (z), height to fin top ~15.8 m.

## Landmarks
Nose cap ≈ z 16.2 … 17.6; wing leading edges |x| > 2.6, low y; cockpit windows ≈ (0, 6, 14). See sts-film/source/story_a.js.
