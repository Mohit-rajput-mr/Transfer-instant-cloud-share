# Two tyres, the same nail (tube vs tubeless, science film)
Suspense science film on the kit (`source/`). Beats: two wheels hit the same nail, one goes flat, one keeps rolling →
the tube tyre: a rubber inner tube holds the air, the tyre is only armour → the tubeless tyre: an airtight inner skin,
edges pressed onto the rim by the air pressure itself → the nail: the tube pops and the air escapes round it and out
through the wheel; the tubeless rubber grips the nail, a slow leak → repair: tyre off vs a plug from outside → no tube
rubbing (less heat, less weight) → why tubes remain (wire-spoke wheels have holes, tubes are cheap) → verdict: tubeless
for everyday roads (fails slowly) → Orbi: nothing in the middle (no login, no app), sealed on your phone, about a second,
30-minute burn.
